About Us

Life Reach Initiative is a non-governmental organisation focused on creating lasting social impact through environmental sanitation campaigns, skill development programs, and youth empowerment initiatives. We believe clean communities build healthy futures.

Our Focus Areas

Environmental Sanitation

Organising community clean-up exercises and promoting proper waste management for healthier neighborhoods.

Youth Empowerment

Training young people in professional cleaning and facility management skills to create employment opportunities.

Student Exam Registration Support

We assist students with WAEC, NECO, and JAMB registration, providing guidance and ensuring smooth academic progress.
